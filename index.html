About

I'm Alec, co-founder and CTO of ThunderPhone. We build AI voice agents that handle real phone calls.

Before that, I founded SensorSurf (YC W23), building data infrastructure for robot fleets, and worked on autonomous systems at Sea Machines and Uber ATG. Earlier still, I built conversational AI for Expedia — chatbots from before LLMs made them easy.

I studied computer science at Ohio State and CU Boulder, where my graduate research in computer vision was published at ECCV 2022. The full record is on the Background page.

I grew up in Cincinnati and currently reside in San Francisco. Outside of work, you'll find me playing the piano, snowboarding, or at church.

I also do genealogy research — generations of Bells, traced and sourced — published as an interactive family tree at Pedigree.

ThunderPhone

AI voice agents that handle real phone calls — answering inbound lines and placing outbound ones — from 2¢ a minute. Alex and I started it together; I run engineering as co-founder and CTO. It's the second company I've built, after SensorSurf (YC W23).
