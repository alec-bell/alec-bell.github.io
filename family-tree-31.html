Eve Sircle held land in her own right in the Shenandoah Valley in the 1750s — fifty-four acres patented to her in September 1755, and in 1761 a hundred and thirty acres at a place called the Bair Glade, bought from John Bair. She was dead by November 1776, when her eldest son Michael conveyed the land as her heir and had the clerk write it down in his own words: “patented to my mother, Eve Sircle, now deceased.” It is the only line in any record that names her. Who her husband was is not known.
